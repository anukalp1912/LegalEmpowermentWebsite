About RightsMitra

We are dedicated to empowering workers across India with free informational guidance and rights awareness.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide free, structured informational guidance to domestic workers, delivery partners, drivers, and daily wage laborers across India.

👥

Our Vision

A world where every worker, regardless of their background or economic status, has access to legal empowerment and can confidently assert their workplace rights.

🤝

Our Commitment

A free hackathon MVP focused on accessible, worker-focused information. It is not a substitute for professional legal advice.

Our Core Values

1

Accessibility

Legal guidance available to all workers, in multiple languages, completely free of charge.

2

4

5

Supporting workers across all sectors - domestic workers, drivers, delivery partners, and more.

Committed to ensuring fair treatment and justice for all workers in the workplace.
