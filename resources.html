Key Labor Laws in India

🏭 Factories Act, 1948

Regulates working conditions in factories, including working hours, safety, welfare provisions, and rest days.

Learn More →

💰 Payment of Wages Act, 1936

Ensures timely payment of wages and prohibits illegal deductions. Covers all workers including daily wage laborers.

Learn More →

📋 Standing Orders Act, 1946

Regulates terms and conditions of employment. Employers must maintain and display written standing orders.

Learn More →

🛡️ Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923

Provides compensation for workplace injuries and occupational diseases. Covers workers in most industries.

Learn More →

💼 Employees' Provident Funds Act, 1952

Establishes provident fund schemes for employee retirement benefits and social security.

Learn More →

🚫 Sexual Harassment Act (POSH), 2013

Protects workers against sexual harassment at workplace. Mandates internal complaints committees.

Learn More →
