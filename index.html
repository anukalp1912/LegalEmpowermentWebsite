Frequently Asked Questions

What is minimum wage in India? +
Minimum wage in India varies by state and sector. As of 2024, the national minimum wage is ₹350 per day for general workers. However, states may have higher rates. Check your local state labor department for specific rates applicable in your area.
Can my employer fire me without notice? +
No. Under Indian labor law, employers must provide proper notice (typically 30 days) or payment in lieu of notice. Sudden dismissal is illegal and you may be entitled to compensation. If you were fired without notice, you can file a complaint with the labor commissioner.
What should I do if my salary is withheld? +
Document the withheld amount and dates. Send a formal written demand to your employer. If unresolved, file a complaint with the District Labor Officer or approach the labor court. You can also contact us for legal guidance.
Am I entitled to leave and holidays? +
Yes. Under the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, workers are entitled to paid leave, sick leave, and holiday benefits. Domestic workers, drivers, and similar categories also have rights to weekly rest and leave. Consult state-specific labor laws for exact entitlements.
