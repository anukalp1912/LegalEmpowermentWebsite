Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a response? +
We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours. For urgent matters, please call our helpline: 1800-WORKER-1.
Is my information completely confidential? +
Yes, absolutely. We follow strict confidentiality protocols. Your personal information is encrypted and never shared with third parties without your consent.
Do you provide professional legal advice? +
We provide general legal guidance based on Indian labor laws. For specific legal advice related to your unique situation, we recommend consulting with a qualified lawyer.
Are your services free? +
Yes, all our services are 100% free. There are no hidden charges or subscription fees. We are committed to providing free legal empowerment to all workers.
What languages do you support? +
We support 8 languages: Hindi, English, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu, Marathi, Kannada, and Malayalam. More languages are being added regularly.
Can I remain anonymous? +
You can use a pseudonym when contacting us. However, providing your actual contact information helps us provide better personalized guidance and follow-up support.
